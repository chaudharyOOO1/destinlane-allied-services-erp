# Payroll, staff remuneration and reliable access

## Internal staff monthly remuneration

Confirmed by Management on 25 October 2026: Management approves staff remuneration each month. No headcount or billing threshold is assumed. An active staff record does not create payroll.

The preparer chooses an active internal staff member, payroll month, gross proposal, reviewed deductions and a branch-work/calculation note. The branch is captured from the staff record. `net_pay = gross_pay − deductions` is generated by PostgreSQL; negative or excess deductions are rejected. Only one proposal per staff/month is allowed.

Management/IT Super Admin decides a DRAFT with a mandatory review note. HR and Accounts cannot approve even with a payroll.approve override. A rejected proposal may be revised and resubmitted as DRAFT. Approved/paid proposals cannot be edited. Payment recording requires approval, a non-future date, reference, appropriate Accounts/Management role and payroll.pay permission. It records a completed payment; it does not initiate a transfer or post an MD journal.

Every proposal, revision, decision and recorded payment retains an audit snapshot. Versions reject stale commands. No production salary amounts have been invented. Staff proposals are reviewed remuneration records; automated statutory deduction setup and statutory filing are not supplied by this feature.

## Deployed employee wages and payroll

Employee wages are entered separately under Payroll. Every wage has an effective start date, positive daily amount, reason and creator. A newer effective date supersedes the earlier rate for later attendance dates. Historical records are retained. Wage changes into finalised periods are rejected.

Completed, verified attendance determines payable days and overtime. Every worked day must have a covering wage rate; otherwise calculation returns 409 without saving a partial run. Calculations are serialized per month. Approved/disbursed slips and finalised runs cannot be recalculated; finalised slips cannot be placed on hold.

Money uses Decimal rounding. The existing employee allowance and statutory percentages remain provisional rules. State/registration/eligibility/cap setup is still required before real employee payroll. This release does not add employee approval/disbursement or post-finalisation adjustment workflows.

## Employee portal

Salary lists read canonical `salary_slips` joined to `payroll_runs`, rather than the separate legacy register. Only approved/disbursed slips appear. PDF downloads use the server renderer and require matching employee ownership; draft/held/other-employee slips return 404.

Profile DTO includes joining date, DOB, father name, address and assigned-site alias. Attendance history/calendar use `attendance_date`. Verified employee documents are retrieved through the authenticated backend, returning five-minute private URLs. Storage paths are not exposed. Expired links are refreshed by reopening Documents. Download errors are visible. The active app and its 404 page no longer use the Base44 SDK; obsolete Base44 authentication/settings screens were removed. Historical Base44 function files remain as unmounted reference material.

## Account permissions

Custom permissions are optional. The editor stages changes and saves one batch. The backend validates every entry before any write, supports null to restore a role default, checks the revision and records an audit entry. Changed access revokes other users' existing sessions. Self-lockout and protected recovery-account restrictions remain enforced.

Office accounts lock for fifteen minutes after five failed attempts. Login ID, email and phone aliases share the account counter across backend instances. Unknown accounts receive constant-cost password verification; this is not a network-wide request limiter.

## Save durability

Legacy invoice, expense, bookkeeping, corporate compliance, salary-register and risk mutations explicitly commit before returning success. Invoice creation supplies issue_date consistently, rejects invalid tax modes/non-finite or negative subtotals, and reconciles split tax to total tax. These operational endpoints remain distinct from MD double-entry bookkeeping.
